Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Beisa Chat does not collect, sell, or share your chat content. AI conversations are processed on your device.

On-Device AI

Beisa Chat may use Apple Intelligence when available or a downloaded local model. Chat content is not sent to Beisa servers.

Network Usage

Network access is used for optional model downloads and App Store system services. Beisa Chat does not include analytics, ads, or third-party tracking.

Microphone

Microphone access is used for voice input when you choose to speak. Audio is processed by system speech services or local components and is not collected by Beisa.
