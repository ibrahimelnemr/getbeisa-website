Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Summary

Beisa does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data from you. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No crash reports are sent
  • No cookies or identifiers
  • No advertising or marketing data
  • No data is shared with third parties

On-Device Processing

Beisa runs an AI language model entirely on your device. All translations — whether from text input, voice, or camera — are processed locally. No text, audio, or images are ever sent to external servers.

Network Usage

Beisa only uses the internet for one purpose: downloading the AI model during initial setup (approximately 3GB). After this one-time download, the app functions completely offline. No other network requests are made.

Camera & Microphone

Beisa requests access to your camera (for OCR translation) and microphone (for voice translation). These inputs are processed entirely on-device and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Data Storage

Translation history is stored locally on your device only. You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the app. We have no access to any data on your device.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no data at all, this applies equally to users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page with a new "Last updated" date. Given that our core principle is zero data collection, we do not anticipate significant changes.
