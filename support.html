Frequently Asked Questions

How much storage does Beisa need?

Beisa downloads a ~3GB AI model during first launch. After that, the app needs approximately 3.5GB total (app + model). Make sure you have enough free space before installing.

Does Beisa work without internet?

Yes! After the initial model download, Beisa works completely offline. You can translate text, voice, and camera content anywhere — no Wi-Fi or cellular connection needed.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. All translations are processed on your device. No text, audio, or images are ever sent to any server. We collect zero data. See our privacy policy for details.

Which languages are supported?

Beisa supports Arabic, French, Spanish, German, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, and more. We're continuously improving language support.

The model download is stuck or failed

Ensure you have a stable internet connection and at least 4GB of free storage. If the download fails, reopen the app — it will resume where it left off. If problems persist, try connecting to a different Wi-Fi network.

Translation quality isn't great

Beisa uses a compact on-device AI model optimized for mobile. While it handles most translations well, complex or highly technical text may not be as accurate as cloud-based services. We're continuously improving the model.
